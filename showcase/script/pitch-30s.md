# Ghost-Prime: the 30-second pitch (for visitors walking past)

**Target:** 30 seconds · about 80 words · smile, and point at the screen.
Every line matches `showcase/FACTS.md` (checked 25 Sep 2026).

---

Hi! I'm **[YOUR NAME]**. **[pause]**

This is **Ghost-Prime**, a voice assistant I built on a school Chromebook.

You just talk to it, and it does the job for you. It opens websites, clicks and types, tidies
files, sets reminders, and reads the answers out loud. **[pause]**

It's for anyone who finds computers hard: slow typers, people who can't read small screens, and
busy teachers. There are no pay-per-use bills.

Want to see it? Tell me a topic, and I'll ask it to look it up on Wikipedia.

---

## Even shorter (10 seconds, for a crowd)

"This is Ghost-Prime. You talk, and it uses the computer for you. I built it on a school
Chromebook. Want to see?"

## For children (they watch, you talk)

"It's a helper that lives inside the computer. I tell it what to do, and it does it. Watch!"

Children watch the screen. They don't speak or type to it. The AI services it uses are for people
aged 18 and over (see `qa.md`, question 16).

---

## Visitor notes (read these once)

- **You speak the request, not the visitor.** Ask them for a topic, then say it into the mic
  yourself, in the same shape as the hero phrase:
  "Go to the Wikipedia website, search for **[their topic]**, and explain it to me in three simple
  sentences." There are two reasons:
  1. Voice recordings go to Google to be turned into text, and on the free tier Google may use them
     to improve its products (FACTS §5). Visitors shouldn't say personal things into it.
  2. Google's free tier has a small daily limit (FACTS §4). A queue of visitors could use it up
     before the judges arrive.
- **Why Wikipedia?** Site access is set to **strict**, and only `wikipedia.org` and `example.com`
  are allowed (FACTS §8, and the setup in `showcase/demo/DEMO.md`). Any other website gets "Site not
  allowed". So keep visitor requests on Wikipedia. If a visitor wants a different site, say: "I've
  locked it to safe sites for today. Let's find it on Wikipedia instead."
- Keep the app in **AUTO** with Site access set to **strict** (FACTS §8). Never leave it in
  **FULL AUTO** while you're away from the table.
- Say no to anything that deletes, buys, posts or logs in, and to anything personal.
- The offline replay (Plan C) only knows the rehearsed phrases. Visitor topics work only in the live
  app.
- If a visitor asks something you can't answer, say: "Good question. I haven't tested that yet."
  Then write it down. That's a perfectly good answer.
- If anyone asks about the phone, it's **built but not tested on a real phone yet**. See `qa.md`,
  question 10.
