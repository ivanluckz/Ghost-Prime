The questions judges ask

Say the dark text, then stop. Grey is for follow-ups.

Chromebooks already have dictation, Select-to-Speak, ChromeVox and Google Assistant. Why this?

Those are good tools, and I’m not trying to replace them. Each one helps with one step at a time. Here, one sentence becomes a whole job: it opens the site, searches, reads, explains, and shows every step.

How much does it cost to run?

No pay-per-use bills. It runs on Google’s free AI plus a Claude subscription I already had, and it can run on the free tier alone (less capable at long tasks).

If asked: With extra usage turned off, Claude pauses at its limit and never charges extra.

Why does every answer say CLAUDE?

Claude does the thinking in today’s demo, so every answer shows a CLAUDE tag. Normally a router can also send quick questions to Google’s free Gemini.

Is it safe?

PLAN only looks. In AUTO, on the Claude brain, an automatic safety check decides each action. FULL AUTO runs everything. There’s no “Are you sure?” pop-up yet.

If asked: The showcase launch starts in AUTO; plain Ghost-Prime starts in FULL AUTO. Single-file changes made with its file tools can be undone.

Have you tested it with the people it’s for? Update if someone tried it

No, not yet. So far I’m the only one who has tested it. I haven’t tested it with these groups yet. That’s my next step, and my ask.

Is it private?

Partly. Chats, memories and files are saved on the Chromebook. Requests, my top memories and the pages it reads go to Anthropic or Google. So don’t tell it anything private.

If asked: Voice goes to Google unless offline voice is on. The free tier may use it to improve Google’s products.

What did YOU build? Edit to what you did

I designed it, decided what it should do, tested it, and used an AI coding assistant (Claude Code) to write much of the code.

If asked: Google and Anthropic make the AI brains. The app and the tools around them are Ghost-Prime.

Is voice tested? What if the free AI runs out? Works · by hand

Voice works in my own testing; the real mic and speaker are only checked by hand. If Google’s free limit runs out, voice is built to switch to offline, but I haven’t tried that for real.

If asked: Today Claude does the thinking and Google does the voice. I can restart in offline voice in about 15 s.

Can it work in Kinyarwanda?

Not yet. The app and the offline speech model are English only. Typing in Kinyarwanda might partly work; I haven’t tested that.

Does the phone part work? In progress

Built, not finished. The Android app passes its tests on a simulated phone. I haven’t run it on my real Galaxy A05 yet. It’s second on my list, after testing with real students and teachers.
